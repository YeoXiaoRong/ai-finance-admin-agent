Welcome to AI Finance Admin Agent

This prototype helps small businesses reduce repetitive invoice administration. You can test how an invoice is calculated, reviewed by a person, finalized and saved as a PDF. No account or API key is needed for the browser-based invoice demonstration.

Follow these steps to test the main workflow

1

Enter invoice details

Use the New Invoice form below. Enter a fictional customer name and email, a service description, quantity, unit price and due date. You can use the sample values provided below.

2

Check the calculations

Review the Subtotal, Discount, Tax and Total Due shown beneath the form. Optionally change the discount type or tax rate to see how the amount changes.

3

Preview before approval

Click Preview & Confirm. A confirmation window displays the invoice details. Check them carefully. Choose Cancel to make changes or continue to approve.

4

Approve and generate

Click Generate Invoice PDF in the confirmation window. This is the human-approval step before invoice finalization. The application attempts to save the invoice and generate its PDF.

5

Review the saved result

After successful creation, use Download PDF in the success window. You can also check Invoice History for the new record and select View PDF when available.
